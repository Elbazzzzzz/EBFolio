The Approach

Research

  • Stakeholder interview programme, run in paired sessions across the charity
  • Competitor analysis of the wider charity sector, extended to cover emotive messaging
  • Thematic analysis distilled into clear, non-technical requirements with assigned success metrics

Design

  • Before-and-after user journey mapping covering internal goals and external users
  • New sitemap, reviewed against an external SEO audit
  • Audience personas combining our research with the charity's own market research
  • Bespoke donation components with quantified impact statements

Outputs

  • Competitor analysis report
  • Persona set with UX, pain-point, and tone of voice guidance
  • Full website redesign, built to WCAG 2.2 AA
  • Ongoing optimisation roadmap as the charity's digital partner

The Brief

Worldwide Cancer Research had just completed a full brand refresh, and this was their first major digital overhaul in some time, so expectations were high both internally and externally. A large group of stakeholders each had a stake in the outcome, and in places our research needed to challenge internal assumptions about what the website could do and what users actually needed. The hardest problem underneath it all: discovery research can take decades to translate into a treatment, so the site had to make impact feel tangible to donors who'd never see a direct product of their gift.

Stakeholder management

I planned and ran the stakeholder interview programme in paired sessions, which kept the process manageable without losing depth. The interviews surfaced both recognised challenges and blind spots stakeholders hadn't considered the website could address, and running them this way built buy-in early. By the time we reached strategy, we had a coalition behind the direction rather than a room of competing agendas, and the playback confirmed the site could go further than the client had expected.

Design decisions

Competitor research showed the sector lacked a playful voice that explained discovery research with real clarity, and that became the consistent thread through design. Bold type, bright colour, 3D elements, and non-intrusive scroll interactions kept the site feeling fresh in a way competitors weren't. The donation component drew a straight line from research to conversion: impact statements quantified what each gift enables, answering the "what does my money actually do" question the interviews had surfaced. I worked closely with developers through build, resolving edge cases as a team and holding the designs to WCAG 2.2 AA.

Outcome

The new site outperformed the old one in both performance and conversion. Donations increased by 225%, traffic grew by 50,000 within three months of launch, and transactions from the homepage rose by 244%. Two years on, we remain the charity's digital partner of choice, contributing to ongoing testing and optimisation including recent conversion journey work that delivered further gains.
