The Walk Wheel Cycle Trust

Full website redesign for a disability charity, clarifying how people donate, access support, and understand the trust’s impact across communities that rely on mobility equipment and services.

The Approach

We started with inclusive research: conversations with beneficiaries, carers, and fundraisers to understand emotional and practical barriers on the legacy site. That fed a phased redesign that balanced warm storytelling with task-led journeys (donate, get help, learn about the trust’s programmes) without overwhelming users with dense content up front.

The Team

A cross-discipline squad of UX, UI, front-end and back-end developers, PM, and client stakeholders. I stayed close to content owners so tone, accessibility, and CMS structure evolved together.

The Problem

The previous experience mixed urgent support pathways with long-form content, making it hard for users under stress to find the right next step. Fundraising narratives also undersold tangible outcomes for donors who wanted transparent, respectful reporting.

Method

Discovery combined interviews and analytics; definition produced revised journeys, sitemap, and component priorities for donations and programme content. Build paired weekly design QA with accessibility checks on real assistive-tech setups.

Discovery

We mapped moments of crisis (equipment failure, new diagnosis) against calmer research tasks (planning fundraising, learning about grants). That contrast shaped navigation labels, cross-links, and the order of reassurance versus action on key templates.

Definition

The sitemap separated evergreen trust stories from seasonal appeals. Donation components gained clearer hierarchy: amount, impact copy, and preferred channels, all tested with users who donate on mobile first.

Design

Visual language stayed bright and human without novelty for its own sake. Spacious layouts, high-contrast type, and predictable UI patterns supported users with varied vision and motor needs while keeping the brand approachable.

Delivery

I supported UAT and training so editors could maintain accessibility in new templates, with structured headings, meaningful link text, and media alternatives, without leaning on developers for every change.

Outcome

Post-launch metrics showed stronger engagement and a meaningful lift in donation income alongside healthier session depth, evidence that clearer pathways and compassionate pacing translated into measurable support for the trust’s mission.
