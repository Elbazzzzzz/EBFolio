Bio

Research-led product designer specialising in accessibility, for charity and social-impact teams. I build design and research processes from scratch and embed them across multidisciplinary teams, working from strategy through to rapid prototypes.

I've led outcomes for users and stakeholders at major charities and NGOs, including Worldwide Cancer Research, Fight for Sight, Marie Curie, and the Global Internet Forum to Counter Terrorism.

Recent projects

Worldwide Cancer Research

Storytelling with a complex charity offering

Full website redesign for a cancer research charity. Restructured the site around distinct donor and researcher journeys, with bespoke donation components, post-donation pathways to encourage monthly giving, and impact storytelling to make discovery research tangible.

+£1,157 / month in donations +18.6% users +19.6% session duration

The Global Internet Forum to Counter Terrorism

High stress use case

A global research tool making terrorism legislation searchable and comparable across jurisdictions. Built to support governments, researchers, and trust-and-safety professionals with different mental models and levels of digital fluency.

Practitioners reported faster legislation research #1 most adopted GIFCT digital release Global summit launch

Designing a Mapping Tool for Disabled Users

Sector leading accessibility research

A research-led mapping tool for pan-impairment active travel, designed with Transport for All and an embedded expert panel of disabled volunteers. Built around real barriers rather than assumed fitness.

Findings report delivered £1mil+ funding unlocked 5 mapping tools audited

+70% donations page traffic 228 newsletter sign-ups / month +£10k donation income in year one
