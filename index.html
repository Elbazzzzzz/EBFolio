I'm a Senior UX Designer turning complex briefs into accessible, research-led products that people can actually use.

As my agency's UX lead for the last five years, I've had the freedom to build research and design processes from scratch and embed them across multidisciplinary project teams. I've been entrusted with outcomes for users and stakeholders at major charities and NGOs worldwide, including Worldwide Cancer Research, Fight for Sight, Marie Curie, and the Global Internet Forum to Counter Terrorism. I care about optimisation, sustainability and accessibility and that forms the basis of all my work.
